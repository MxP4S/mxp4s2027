# Organizers

The MxP4S 2027 organizing committee brings together researchers and practitioners from academia, national laboratories, and industry. The organizers' expertise includes numerical linear algebra, mixed-precision algorithms, scientific applications, GPU computing, system benchmarking, and emerging HPC architectures.

## Aditya Kashi

**Oak Ridge National Laboratory, USA**

Aditya Kashi is a computer scientist at Oak Ridge National Laboratory. His research focuses on sparse linear algebra on GPUs, large-scale benchmarking, and mixed-precision numerical methods for scientific computing. He served on the ISC-HPC 2022 workshop committee and co-organized the HCLH workshop at SC26.

## Piotr Luszczek

**MIT Lincoln Laboratory and the University of Tennessee, USA**

Piotr Luszczek is a member of the technical staff at MIT Lincoln Laboratory, a Research Associate Professor at the University of Tennessee, and a member of the Innovative Computing Laboratory. His research includes numerical linear algebra, mixed-precision iterative refinement, benchmarking, automatic performance tuning, and performance modeling. He serves as Editor-in-Chief of ACM Transactions on Mathematical Software and as a member of the TOP500 Operations Committee.

## Hao Lu

**Oak Ridge National Laboratory, USA**

Hao Lu is a research scientist at Oak Ridge National Laboratory. His research focuses on scalable algorithms for large-scale graph analysis and mixed-precision dense linear algebra, including Graph500, HPL, and HPL-MxP. He co-organized the HCLH workshop at SC26.

## Paul Lin

**NVIDIA, USA**

Paul Lin is a Solutions Architect at NVIDIA. His expertise includes numerical methods for computational fluid dynamics and plasma physics, linear algebra solvers, application performance optimization, and the procurement of next-generation supercomputers. He has served on the program committees of major HPC conferences and co-organized the HCLH workshop at SC26.

## Toshiyuki Imamura

**RIKEN Center for Computational Science, Japan**

Toshiyuki Imamura leads the Large-Scale Parallel Numerical Computing Technology Team at RIKEN R-CCS. His research focuses on numerical algorithms and libraries for emerging HPC systems, including FugakuNEXT. His recent work includes high-accuracy GEMM emulation using Ozaki Scheme I and Ozaki Scheme II.

## Erin Carson

**Charles University, Czechia**

Erin Carson is an Associate Professor in the Faculty of Mathematics and Physics at Charles University. Her research focuses on matrix computations, parallel numerical algorithms, and finite-precision behavior on modern heterogeneous hardware. She received the 2025 Wilkinson Prize in Numerical Analysis and Scientific Computing and serves in leadership and editorial roles across several international numerical computing organizations.
